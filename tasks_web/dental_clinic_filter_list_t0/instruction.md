The treatment index halfway down our site lists all twelve things the practice
does, and above it sits a row of category buttons — All, Preventive, Cosmetic,
Restorative and Emergency — so that someone who just wants to know what a
filling costs is not reading through the whitening and the veneers to find it.

Pressing one of those buttons does mark it as the chosen one, but nothing else
happens. All twelve cards stay on the page in the same order whichever category
is pressed, and the line above them still reads "Showing 12 of 12 treatments"
every time. Patients end up ringing the front desk to ask which of the twelve
applies to them, which is the exact phone call the index was meant to save us.

Choosing a category should leave only that category's treatments on the page,
with the line above the cards reporting how many are actually showing, and
choosing All should bring the full twelve back. The page should still open on
All with everything visible, the way it does now.

Nothing else should change: the same twelve treatments, with the same prices,
chair times and wording, in the same order, and the rest of the page as it is.
