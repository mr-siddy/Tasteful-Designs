The "Reserve your workspace in about forty seconds" block on our marketing site
is losing every signup, and it took us a week to work out why: you cannot type
into it. Click into the workspace name, type "Northwind Platform", and the field
stays empty — the letters never appear at all. Same with the work email, same
with the notes box. The little character counter above the name sits at zero no
matter how long you hold a key down.

The panel beside the form is meant to be a live preview of what the team will
see, and it never moves either: it keeps offering postlude.app/your-workspace
and says the invite is going to nobody yet, whoever is filling the form in. And
because none of it registers, pressing "Reserve this workspace" comes back
telling the visitor we need a name and a work address — which is exactly what
they just typed.

What should happen is the ordinary thing: every keystroke shows up in the field
it was typed into, each field keeps its own text while you fill in the others,
clearing a field and typing again replaces what was there, the preview follows
along as you go, and reserving confirms the workspace using the name and address
that were actually entered.

Nothing else on the page should change — same sections in the same order, same
copy, same fields with the same labels, same validation for a genuinely empty or
malformed entry.
