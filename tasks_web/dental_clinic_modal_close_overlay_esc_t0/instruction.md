Marisol at the front desk has been fielding complaints about the booking popup
on our practice site. When someone presses "Reserve a new-patient visit" the
request form comes up over a dimmed page, which is fine — but then it will not
go away. Clicking the dark area around it does nothing. Hitting Escape does
nothing. The only thing that dismisses it is the small close control in its top
corner, and a fair number of people never spot that: they think the page has
frozen, and one patient told us she gave up and phoned instead.

The popup should behave the way every other one on the web does. Clicking the
dimmed backdrop behind it should put it away, and so should the Escape key, on
top of the close control that already works. A click that lands on the popup
itself — on a form field, on the heading, anywhere inside the panel — must not
dismiss it; people do that constantly while filling something in and losing what
they have typed would be worse than the current problem. Dismissing it should
leave the page underneath exactly as it was, and pressing the button again
should bring it straight back.

Nothing else about the site should change: same sections, same copy, same form
fields inside the popup, same close control.
