Foundry Row's membership section puts three tiers side by side — Open Floor,
Coached and Full Program — and the club promotes the middle one. Coached is what
the coaches quote first and where about two thirds of new members land, so it is
the tier that is meant to wear the "Most popular" tag and the raised, dark card
treatment. Open Floor and Full Program are supposed to sit plain on either side
of it.

That is not what the page shows. The tag is sitting on Full Program, the $289
tier, so the most expensive membership reads as the one everybody picks and the
tier we actually want people on looks like the middle option nobody chose. Front
desk has already had two walk-ins ask why the premium plan is the popular one.
Flipping the billing toggle to annual and back doesn't move it either — the
prices change but the tag stays where it is.

Exactly one card on the page should carry that tag, it should be Coached, and it
should stay on Coached under both monthly and annual billing.

Leave everything else as it is: the same three tiers in the same order, the same
prices, features and commitment terms, the billing toggle behaving the way it
does now, and the rest of the page untouched.
