Patients keep telling reception the site is unusable on a phone, and they are right.

Tap the menu button in the top corner and the list of destinations slides over the
page — treatments, same-day crowns, our dentists, membership, hours and parking.
Pick one of them and it does take you down to the right part of the page, but the
list stays sitting there on top of it, and the dimmed layer behind it stays too.
You end up reading the practice through a dark sheet with five links printed over
it, and the only way out is to go back up and hit the X, or the menu button again.
Two people this week gave up and phoned instead. The booking button inside that
list behaves the same way.

Choosing somewhere to go should send you there and put the menu away — the panel
and the dimmed layer both gone, the page clear underneath, and the menu button
back to reporting itself as closed for anyone using a screen reader. Tapping the
menu button afterwards should still bring the same five destinations back.

Nothing else about the site should change: the same destinations in the same
order, the same phone number and booking button inside the menu, the X still
there for anyone who opens the menu and changes their mind, and every other
section of the page exactly as it is.
