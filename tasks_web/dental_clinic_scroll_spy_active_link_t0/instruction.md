Our site is one very long single page, so the row of links across the top of the
header — Treatments, Technology, Your visit, Membership, Patients, Questions — is
the only way anyone can tell where they are in it. It is supposed to keep pace
with the reader: as each of those six sections comes up under the top of the
window, that section's link should be the one standing out, and it should hand
over to the next one as you carry on down.

It never does. Scroll the whole way from the hero to the questions at the bottom
and every link stays exactly as grey as it was when the page loaded, so by the
time you are somewhere in the middle of the treatment prices you have no idea how
far through you are or how much is left. Scrolling back up is no better. A patient
who uses a screen reader gets nothing either — the nav never announces any of the
six as the one they are currently in.

What it should do: exactly one of those six links is marked as the current
section at any moment, it is the section actually sitting at the top of the
window, it is visibly different from the other five, and it is announced as the
current one to assistive technology. It has to follow the reader upward as well
as downward, and jumping straight from the top of the page to the bottom should
land the highlight on the right link too.

Nothing else about the page should change: the same six destinations in the same
order pointing at the same sections, and every other section left exactly as it
is.
