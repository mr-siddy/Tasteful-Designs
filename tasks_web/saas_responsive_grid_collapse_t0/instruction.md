Roughly two thirds of the people who reach our marketing site do it from a phone
— usually a floor lead standing next to a spreader, holding the phone in one
hand. So the section in the middle of the page that lays out the six things
Grainline does is the part that has to survive a small screen, and right now it
does not.

On a phone those six cards are squeezed two across. Each one ends up about the
width of a business card, the headings break over three lines, and the body copy
turns into a narrow ribbon of two or three words per line that nobody reads.
Someone on a sales call last week described it as "the confetti section". On a
laptop it looks fine, which is why it went out like this.

What we want is what every other card section on the site already does: on a
phone the cards should stack, one full-width card per row, so each one is
actually readable. From tablet width up they should sit two across, and on a
laptop or wider, three across the row the way they do today.

Nothing else should change — the same six cards in the same order, the same
headings, copy and icons, the same spacing between them, and every other section
of the page exactly as it is.
