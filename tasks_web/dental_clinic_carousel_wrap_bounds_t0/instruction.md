About a third of the way down the practice site there is a "In their words"
section with five patient reviews in a slider — a back arrow, a next arrow, a
"1 of 5" counter and a row of five dots to jump straight to one.

It works right up until you reach the end of it. Press next on the fifth review
and the whole panel empties: no quote, no name, just the arrows sitting under a
blank white box, and the counter reading "6 of 5". Press it again and it says
"7 of 5". The same thing happens going the other way — pressing back on the very
first review empties the panel and the counter reads "0 of 5". Once it is empty
none of the dots are marked any more either, so a patient has no idea where in
the five they have ended up. Two people have emailed reception this month
thinking the reviews had been taken down.

Reaching either end of the reviews should never leave the slider showing
nothing. Whichever way someone is pressing, there should always be exactly one
review on the panel, the counter should always read somewhere between one and
five, and the dot for whichever review is showing should be the one marked. The
dots imply the five run in a loop, and that is how people expect a set of
reviews like this to behave.

Leave the reviews themselves alone — the same five quotes, the same names, jobs
and treatments, in the same order — and leave the rest of the page exactly as it
is.
