The menu block in the middle of our site prints all fourteen dishes we are
serving tonight, and above the grid there is a row of buttons — All dishes, Small
plates, From the hearth, Pasta & grains, Sweets — so somebody who has already
decided they want a plate of pasta can pull those three out and stop reading
about trout.

The buttons do not work. Press Sweets and the button goes dark as though it has
taken the choice, and then nothing else on the page moves: all fourteen cards are
still sitting in the grid, cornbread and pork chop and everything else, in the
same order they were in before. The little summary line above the grid is just as
stuck — it says "Showing 14 of 14 dishes" whichever button you press, so it never
tells you anything either. Two guests this week have phoned to ask whether we
still do a dessert, having pressed Sweets and given up on the wall of dishes.

What should happen is the plain thing the buttons promise. Choosing a course
should leave only that course's dishes in the grid and take the rest away — Sweets
shows the two puddings and nothing else, From the hearth shows the four dishes
that go over the fire — and choosing All dishes should put the full fourteen back.
The summary line should always agree with what a guest can count on the screen.

Nothing else about the page should change: the same fourteen dishes with the same
names, prices, dietary notes and descriptions in the same order, the same five
buttons with the same labels in the same order, and every other section of the
page exactly as it is.
