Near the bottom of our restaurant page there is a block called "The seven things
people ring us about" — seven questions you click to read the answer to. It is
supposed to behave the way these things normally do: you open one, read it, and
when you open the next one the previous answer folds itself away.

It does not. Every question you click stays open. Someone checking whether we take
walk-ins, then where to park, then whether they can bring the kids ends up with
three answers stacked on the page at once, and the questions they have not read
yet have been pushed the better part of a screen further down. Work through the
whole list and all seven are hanging open, with no way to tidy it up except going
back and clicking each one shut. The little plus icons agree with the mess —
several of them show as open at the same time.

Opening a question should show that one answer and close whichever answer was
already open, so there is never more than one on screen. Clicking the question
that is currently open should still shut it and leave nothing expanded, and the
block should still load with every answer collapsed, the way it does now.

Don't change the questions or the answers themselves — same seven, same wording,
same order — and leave the rest of the page alone.
