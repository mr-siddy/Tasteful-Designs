The "Member stories" band on our landing page shows one member at a time, and you
page through the five of them with the arrow buttons on the right. It works right
up until the end: press the forward arrow once past Priscilla, the last member, and
the whole card goes blank — a big empty panel where the quote was, none of the five
dots lit, and the counter underneath reading "6 / 5". Keep pressing and it just
counts higher into nothing. The backward arrow does the same thing at the other end:
press it on the very first member and you get the same empty card and a counter
reading "0 / 5". The only way out is to reload the page or click one of the dots.

Two people have emailed us about it this month, and it is the section we point new
members at, so it needs to be the section that works.

Paging forward from the last member, or backward from the first, should always land
on a real member story — carry on round to the other end of the set, or simply stay
put on the one that is showing. Whatever it lands on, the card, the highlighted dot
and the "n / 5" counter should all agree, and the counter should never leave the
range of members we actually have.

Nothing else about the page should change: the same five members in the same order
with the same quotes, the dots and the counter where they are, and every other
section left alone.
