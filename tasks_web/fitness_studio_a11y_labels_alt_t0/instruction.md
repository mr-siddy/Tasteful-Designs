One of our members has been blind since her twenties and does everything on her
phone with VoiceOver. She offered to walk the new club page before we put the
address on the window, and she could not get through it.

The photographs came out as nothing at all. The big one under "A coal depot with
five rooms in it" and the five smaller ones beneath it were passed over in
silence, so she never learned that we had shown her the Platform Room, the Rack
Wall, the Engine Room, the Rebuild Studio or the Boiler Room — which is most of
what that part of the page exists to do. The four coach portraits are the same:
she heard Nadia's, Marcus's, Priya's and Tomás's names in the text, but the
pictures beside them were skipped, so as far as her reader was concerned there
were no people on the page at all.

The little round controls in the top right corner were worse. Her reader
announced each of the four as just "button", one after another, so she had to
press all four blind to work out which one shows this week's timetable, which one
is the front desk number, which one is directions and parking, and which one is
her membership. The two round arrows beside the big photograph have the same
problem — a pair of unnamed buttons and no way to know which one moves to the
next room and which one goes back.

The booking panel at the bottom is where she gave up. She could hear "Your name",
"Email address", "Mobile number", "What you want out of the first eight weeks",
"Days a week you can train", "Anything a coach should know" and the Sunday
timetable opt-in read out as she moved down the panel, but the moment she landed
in one of the boxes her reader said nothing — so she could not tell which of the
seven she was about to type into, and she closed the page without booking an
intro.

All of it should be announceable. Every photograph should say what it is showing,
including the big one, which should announce whichever room is on display at the
time. Every control that is nothing but an icon should say what it does. And
every box in the booking panel should be tied to the wording already printed
beside it, so that landing in a box announces the right thing.

This is about what a screen reader says, not about how the page looks. Keep the
layout, the copy, the photographs, the controls, the options and the fields
exactly as they are, and leave the rest of the page alone.
