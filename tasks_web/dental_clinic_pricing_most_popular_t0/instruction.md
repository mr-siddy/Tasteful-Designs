Our membership section offers three plans — Bright Start for a healthy adult who
just needs two cleanings a year, Complete Care for most people, and Whole Family
for up to four people at one address. Complete Care is the one we promote. It is
what Dr. Raghunathan recommends at the end of a first visit, it is where about
two thirds of new members land, and on the page it is meant to be the obvious
middle choice: the raised, darker card wearing the "Most popular" tag.

That is not what visitors are seeing. The tag, and the raised treatment that goes
with it, are sitting on Whole Family — the last card in the row, the household
plan at $118 a month — while Complete Care renders as the plain, quiet card in
the middle. Sasha has taken three calls this month from single patients
apologising for not having a family to sign up, and one man asked whether he
could pay the family rate on his own because he assumed the promoted plan was the
only one worth having. Switching between the monthly and yearly rate makes no
difference: the tag stays where it is either way.

Exactly one membership should carry that promotion, and it should be Complete
Care — the tag appears once on the whole page, inside the Complete Care card, and
that is the card that gets the raised treatment while Bright Start and Whole
Family stay plain. It should stay that way whichever billing cadence the visitor
is looking at.

Nothing else about the site should change: the same three memberships in the same
order at the same prices, the same inclusions and join buttons, the same
monthly/yearly toggle behaviour, and every other section of the page exactly as
it is.
