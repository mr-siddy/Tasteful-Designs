Our pricing section sells three plans — Solo for a single truck, Crew for shops
running up to eight, and Fleet for the multi-branch operations. Crew is the one
we promote. It is the plan roughly two thirds of new shops land on, it is what
the sales team quotes first, and on the page it is supposed to be the obvious
middle choice: the dark card that sits slightly proud of the other two, wearing
the "Most popular" tag.

That is not what visitors see. The tag and the dark treatment are on the two
plans either side of it — Solo and Fleet both look like the promoted option —
and Crew is rendered as the plain, quiet card between them. Two shops called
this week asking why the cheapest plan and the most expensive plan are both the
most popular one, and an owner told us he nearly signed up for Solo because it
looked like the plan we were pointing at. Switching the billing toggle between
monthly and annual does not change anything about it either way.

Exactly one plan should carry that promotion, and it should be Crew: the tag
appears once on the whole page, inside the Crew card, and Crew is the card that
gets the raised, dark treatment while Solo and Fleet stay plain. It should stay
that way no matter which billing period the visitor is looking at.

Nothing else about the site should change: the same three plans in the same
order with the same prices, features and buttons, the same billing toggle
behaviour, and every other section of the page exactly as it is.
