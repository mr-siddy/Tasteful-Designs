Our pricing section is supposed to steer people to the Team plan — it's the tier
we recommend, the one the sales copy leans on, and the middle of the three cards.

Right now the "Most popular" ribbon is sitting on every card. Starter, Team and
Scale all wear it, and all three get the same accented border and the same filled
button, so the row reads as three identical shouts and nothing stands out. Two
prospects this week have written in to ask which plan we actually recommend,
which is exactly the question the section was meant to answer.

Only the Team plan should be called out: one ribbon, on the middle tier, with the
highlight treatment that goes with it. Starter and Scale should sit as plain
cards with no ribbon.

Leave the plans themselves alone — same names, prices, blurbs, features and
order — and don't change anything else on the page.
