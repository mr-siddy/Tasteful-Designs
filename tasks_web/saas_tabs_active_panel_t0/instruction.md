Halfway down our marketing page there is a block called "One workspace, four
jobs" where visitors pick between the four things Weirline does — Sampling, Lab
results, Reporting and Audits — using the row of buttons above the write-up.
Clicking one does darken that button, but it doesn't actually put that workflow
in front of the reader: all four write-ups are still printed one after another
down the page, sampling then lab results then reporting then audits, each with
its own diagram. Someone who clicks "Reporting" is looking at the sampling
calendar, and on a phone the block is four screens of scrolling instead of one.

Choosing a workflow should show that workflow and nothing else, with the page
opening on Sampling the way it does now. Someone using a screen reader should
also be able to tell which of the four is currently showing — right now it
announces every one of them as though it were the chosen one.

Leave the workflows themselves alone: the same headings, write-ups, capability
lines, diagrams and numbers, in the same order, and the rest of the page
unchanged.
