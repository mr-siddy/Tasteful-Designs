Down near the bottom of our marketing site there is a "Questions publishers ask
us first" block — seven questions a visitor clicks to read the answer to before
they book a walkthrough. It is supposed to behave the way these things always do:
you open one, you read it, and when you open the next one the previous answer
folds itself back up.

It doesn't. Every question you click stays open. Click the one about our
spreadsheet import, then the one about who owns the metadata, then the one about
printers, and now there are three answers stacked on the page at once and the
remaining questions have been shoved most of a screen further down. Someone
working through the list ends up with all seven expanded and a wall of text they
have to click shut one at a time. The little plus icons agree with the mess —
several of them read as open simultaneously, and a screen reader announces four
or five questions as expanded when the visitor has only ever been reading one.

Opening a question should show that one answer and close whichever answer was
already open, so there is never more than one on the page. The block should still
load with everything collapsed the way it does now, and clicking the question
that is currently open should still close it and leave nothing expanded.

Don't touch the questions or the answers themselves — same seven, same wording,
same order — and leave the rest of the page exactly as it is.
