The two-week trial pass form is the only thing on the Ashgrove Barbell Club page
that we actually need to work, and right now nobody can fill it in.

Click into "Full name" and type. The cursor blinks, the keyboard does something,
and the field stays empty — every character disappears the instant you type it.
Same in the email box, the mobile box and the long "what do you want out of the
next eight weeks" box. The trial pass card sitting beside the form is supposed to
fill itself in as you go, so you can see your own name printed on the pass before
you send it; it just sits there on its dashes. The little countdown under the
goal box still says there are 240 characters left no matter how much you write.
And because the "Book my trial fortnight" button only unlocks once there is a
name and an email in the form, it never unlocks at all — it stays greyed out
forever and the form cannot be sent. Priya has had three people ring the desk
this week to say the website is broken.

What should happen is the ordinary thing: what you type into a field is what the
field shows, and it stays there while you fill in the rest. The pass preview
should track the name, the email and the goal as they are typed, the countdown
should tick down as the goal is written, and once a name and an email are both
present the button should become clickable and submitting should confirm the
booking back to the visitor by name.

Keep the form exactly as it is otherwise — same five fields in the same order,
same labels, same wording on the button, same preview card and the same list of
what the fortnight includes — and leave the rest of the page alone.
