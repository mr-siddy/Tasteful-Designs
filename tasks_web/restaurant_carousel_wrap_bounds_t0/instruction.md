The "Guest stories" section of our site shows one guest's quote at a time, with
back and forward arrows underneath, a little counter that reads "1 / 4", and a
row of four dots. Stepping forward through the four stories works fine — until
you reach the last one. Press forward once more on Owen's story and the quote,
the name and the portrait all vanish: the panel is left empty with the arrows
and dots still sitting under it, the counter reads "5 / 4", and none of the dots
are lit any more. Pressing back from the very first story does the same thing,
and once the slider is blank the only way out is to click one of the dots or
reload the page. Two people this week have told us the testimonials section is
broken on their phone.

Forward from the last story and back from the first should both land the guest
on a real story rather than on an empty panel — the section should never show a
guest count outside the four stories we have, and the lit dot and the counter
should always agree with whichever quote is on screen. Stepping forward through
all four in order, and jumping straight to a story by clicking its dot, should
keep working exactly as they do now.

The four quotes, the names, the attributions and the rest of the page should be
left exactly as they are.
