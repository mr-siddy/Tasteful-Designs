The menu section of our site offers three menus — Lunch, Dinner and Drinks —
and guests pick between them with the row of buttons above the dishes. Picking
one does highlight it, but it doesn't actually put that menu in front of them:
all three are still printed one after another down the page, lunch then dinner
then the drinks list. Guests scroll past everything to find the one they wanted,
and on a phone the section is an endless wall of dishes.

Choosing a menu should show that menu and nothing else, with the page opening on
the lunch menu the way it does now. Someone using a screen reader should also be
able to tell which of the three is currently showing — at the moment it announces
all of them as though they were chosen.

Leave the menus themselves alone: the same dishes, notes and prices, in the same
order, and the rest of the page unchanged.
