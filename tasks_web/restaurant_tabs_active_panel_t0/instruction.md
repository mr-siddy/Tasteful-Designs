Our menu section offers four menus — Lunch, Dinner, Bar and Sunday Rice — and
guests choose between them with the row of buttons above the dishes. Pressing one
does light it up, but it never actually puts that menu in front of anyone: all
four are still printed one after another down the page, lunch then dinner then
the bar list then Sunday. Guests scroll past everything they didn't ask for to
reach the one they wanted, and on a phone the section is an endless wall of
dishes. Two people have now ordered off Sunday's rice list on a Wednesday.

Choosing a menu should put that menu on the page and take the others off it, with
the section opening on Lunch the way it does now. Someone using a screen reader
should also be able to hear which of the four is the one currently showing — at
the moment every button announces itself as the chosen one.

Leave the menus themselves alone: the same dishes, notes and prices, in the same
order, and the rest of the page exactly as it is.
