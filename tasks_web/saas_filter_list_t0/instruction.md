Our landing page has a template library near the middle of the page: a row of
category buttons — All, Sales, Finance, Support and Security — sitting above a
grid of twelve workflow templates.

The buttons look like they work. Press Finance and it darkens the way the
selected one should, and the line above the grid still reads "Showing 12 of 12
templates". But the grid underneath never changes: all twelve templates stay on
screen, in the same order, whichever category you press. A visitor who came
looking for the finance workflows is left scrolling past sales and support
cards, and on a phone it is a very long scroll to find out we never filtered
anything.

Choosing a category should leave only the templates in that category on the
grid — nothing from the other categories — and the count line above it should
say how many are actually showing. Choosing All should bring the whole library
of twelve back. The page should still open on All, showing everything, the way
it does now.

Nothing else about the page should change: the same twelve templates with the
same names, descriptions and counts, in the same order, and every other section
left as it is.
