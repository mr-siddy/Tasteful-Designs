Most of the bookings for our £9 intro ride start with the button in the middle of
the page, which brings up a short request form over the top of everything else.
The form itself is fine — people fill it in and it sends. The trouble is getting
out of it.

Once that panel is up, the only way past it is the small ✕ in the corner. Tapping
the dimmed area around it does nothing, and pressing Escape does nothing either,
so anyone who opened it to have a look is stuck staring at a form they did not
want. Marta says people ring the desk asking how to get back to the timetable,
and on a phone, where the ✕ sits up in the corner under the notch, several have
just closed the tab instead.

Opening the panel should behave the way people expect a panel over a page to
behave: a click on the dimmed area behind it puts it away, and so does Escape,
and either one should work every time it is opened, not only the first time. A
click that lands on the form itself — a field, a label, the heading — must leave
it open, and the ✕ should keep working exactly as it does now.

Nothing else about the page should change: same intro times, same fields in the
same order, same copy everywhere else.
