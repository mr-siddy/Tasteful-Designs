Near the bottom of the practice page there is a block headed "Questions the front
desk hears every week" — seven questions you click to read the answer to. It is
supposed to behave the way these lists normally do: you open one, you read it,
and when you move on to the next one the answer you just finished folds itself
back up.

It doesn't. Every question you click stays open. Click the one about knowing the
cost, then the one about being frightened of the chair, then the one about
parking, and all three answers are sitting on the page at once with the remaining
questions shoved most of a screen further down. Someone reading through the list
ends up with all seven expanded and a wall of text, and the only way to tidy it up
is to click each one shut again. The little plus icons agree with the mess —
several of them are showing as open at the same time, so a screen reader announces
several expanded questions at once.

Opening a question should show that one answer and close whichever answer was
already open, so there is never more than one on screen. Clicking the question
that is already open should still close it and leave nothing expanded, and the
block should still load with all seven collapsed the way it does now.

Don't touch the questions or the answers themselves — same seven, same wording,
same order — and leave the rest of the page alone.
