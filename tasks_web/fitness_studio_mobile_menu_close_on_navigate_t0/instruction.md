Almost everyone who books an intro session at the studio does it on their phone,
and on a phone our five destinations live behind the little menu button in the
header. Tapping that button slides the list of destinations down over the page —
Classes, Timetable, Method, Instructors, Memberships, plus the booking button and
the front desk number — which is exactly what we wanted.

The problem is what happens next. A visitor taps "Timetable", the page does jump
down to the timetable, but the list is still sitting there on top of it covering
the thing they just asked to see. The same happens for every destination in the
list and for the booking button inside it. The only way to get rid of it is to
find the small × and tap that as well, and most people don't — they scroll around
underneath the list, decide the site is broken, and leave. On a wide screen,
where the destinations sit along the top of the header instead, nothing is wrong.

Choosing a destination on a phone should take the visitor there *and* put the
list away, so they are looking at the section they picked. The menu button should
go back to reading as closed once that has happened, and tapping it again should
bring the same list back exactly as before.

Nothing else should change: the same five destinations in the same order, the
same booking button and phone number in the list, the × should keep working, the
wide-screen header should be untouched, and the rest of the page — classes,
timetable, memberships, instructors, the FAQ, the footer — should stay exactly as
it is.
