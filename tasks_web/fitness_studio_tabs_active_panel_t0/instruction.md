Our programme section offers three training tracks — Row, Strength and Restore —
and visitors are meant to pick between them with the row of buttons above the
sessions. Picking one does highlight the button, but it doesn't put that track in
front of them: all three are still printed one under the other down the page, the
row sessions then the strength sessions then the recovery ones. People scroll
through twelve sessions to find the four they came for, and on a phone the
section is an endless column of cards.

Choosing a track should show that track's sessions and nothing else, with the
page opening on Row the way it does now. Someone using a screen reader should
also be able to tell which of the three is currently showing — at the moment it
announces all three as though every one of them had been chosen.

Leave the tracks themselves alone: the same sessions, lengths, targets and
descriptions, in the same order, and the rest of the page unchanged.
