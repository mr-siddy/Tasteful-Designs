Members book almost everything from their phones, and the timetable section of
our site — "This week on the floor" — is unusable on one. The six class cards
are crammed side by side into three narrow columns no matter how small the screen
gets, so "Saturday Barbell Club" wraps down one word per line, the coach line
overflows, and reading the week means pinching and scrolling sideways. The four
intensity tiles underneath it are worse: four slivers barely wide enough for the
word "Compete".

On a phone the cards in that section should sit one under the other, each one
full width and comfortably readable, and only spread out into several columns
once there is real room for them — on a tablet and up — the way the rest of the
page already behaves when you narrow the window. On a laptop the section should
look exactly as it does today.

Nothing else should change: the same six classes with the same days, times,
coaches and descriptions, the same four intensity tags, and every other section
of the page left alone.
