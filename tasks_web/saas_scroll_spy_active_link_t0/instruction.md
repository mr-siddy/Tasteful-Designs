Our marketing site is one long scrolling page, and the nav in the sticky header
is meant to double as a "you are here" rail: as you read down the page, the link
for the section currently on screen should stand out from the others, and should
be the one announced as current to anyone using a screen reader.

It doesn't work. Scroll from the hero all the way down to the FAQ and the nav
never changes — every destination stays the same muted grey the entire way, so
on a page this long there is nothing telling a reader where they are or how much
is left. Our head of marketing did notice one exception while filming a walk-
through: the very first destination, Platform, does light up when you reach it.
Everything past that point is dead, and once you have scrolled on from Platform
the highlight is stuck there rather than following you.

Reading a section should mark its link, and only its link, as the current one —
visibly and for assistive tech — and that mark should move along as the reader
keeps scrolling down the page.

Nothing else should change: the same sections in the same order with the same
copy, and the links should still jump to their sections when clicked.
