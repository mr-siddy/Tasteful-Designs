The customer stories section on our landing page shows one story at a time —
five organisations, with the arrows underneath to move between them and a row of
dots showing where you are. It reads fine until you get to the end of it.

Press the forward arrow one more time on the fifth story and the whole panel
empties out. The quote, the name, the numbers, all of it disappears and you are
left looking at a blank box with the arrows still sitting under it. The little
line beside the heading keeps counting up too — it starts saying "Story 6 of 5",
then seven of five, and none of the dots is lit any more because there is nothing
to be on. Keep pressing and it never comes back; you have to reload the page or
click one of the dots to get a story again. The same thing happens straight away
at the other end: on the very first story, pressing the back arrow empties it
just as fast.

We noticed because a funder we had sent the page to mentioned she thought the
site had broken halfway through reading the testimonials, which is not the
impression we were going for.

The arrows should just loop. Going forward from the last story should bring you
back round to the first one, and going back from the first should take you to
the last, so there is always a story on screen, the counter always reads
somewhere between one and five, and the lit dot always matches whatever you are
reading. However many times someone leans on either arrow, they should never be
able to push it off the end of the set.

Nothing else about the page should change: the same five stories with the same
quotes, names and figures in the same order, the dots still jumping straight to
a story when clicked, and every other section exactly as it is.
