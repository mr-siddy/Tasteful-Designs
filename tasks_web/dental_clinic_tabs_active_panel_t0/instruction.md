Halfway down our practice site there is a "Care pathways" section that lets
people pick between the four routes through the clinic — Preventive, Cosmetic,
Restorative and Emergency. Choosing one does colour the button in, so it looks
like it worked, but the page underneath never changes: all four pathways are
printed one after another down the screen, preventive first, then cosmetic, then
restorative, then emergency. Someone who wanted to know what whitening costs
scrolls through sixteen treatments to find it, and on a phone the section is a
wall of text nobody reads to the bottom of.

Picking a pathway should put that pathway in front of them and nothing else,
with the page opening on Preventive the way it does now. A patient using a
screen reader has the same problem in a worse form — the four choices are all
announced as though they were the one currently chosen, so there is no way to
tell which pathway is actually showing.

Leave the pathways themselves alone: the same treatments, notes, prices and
footnotes, in the same order, and the rest of the page exactly as it is.
