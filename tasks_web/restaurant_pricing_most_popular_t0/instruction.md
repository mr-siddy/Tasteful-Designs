Our private dining section offers three packages — Hearthside for a small party in
the back room, the Chef's Table for up to twenty-four with Margo cooking at the
hearth rail, and Full House when somebody takes the whole restaurant. The Chef's
Table is the one we promote. It is what roughly two thirds of private bookings
land on, it is the package Delphine quotes first on the events line, and on the
page it is meant to be the obvious middle choice: the raised, outlined card
wearing the "Most popular" tag.

That is not what visitors are seeing. The tag, and the raised treatment that goes
with it, are sitting on Full House — the last card in the row, the buyout, the
one that costs the most per guest — while the Chef's Table renders as the plain,
quiet card in the middle. Two enquiries this week opened with an apology for not
being able to afford "the popular one", and a couple planning an anniversary
dinner for twenty told Delphine they had assumed the Chef's Table was the
leftover option. Flipping between the weeknight and weekend rate makes no
difference: the tag stays where it is either way.

Exactly one package should carry that promotion, and it should be the Chef's
Table — the tag appears once on the whole page, inside the Chef's Table card, and
that is the card that gets the raised treatment while Hearthside and Full House
stay plain. It should stay that way whichever rate the visitor is looking at.

Nothing else about the site should change: the same three packages in the same
order with the same per-guest prices, the same inclusions and enquiry buttons,
the same weeknight/weekend toggle behaviour, and every other section of the page
exactly as it is.
