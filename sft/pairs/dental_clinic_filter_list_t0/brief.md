# Brief — Tidewater Dental Studio (dental clinic, landing page)

Build the marketing landing page for **Tidewater Dental Studio**, an independent
practice at 40 Cobb Street in the Marlowe Quarter, opened in 2013 by
Dr. Nadia Kwarteng and now four clinicians strong. The practice's entire pitch is
**the published catalogue**: every treatment it offers is listed on the website
with a real price and a real chair time, so nobody is quoted a number for the
first time while they are lying down. Its second differentiator is the in-house
ceramic mill — a crown designed, milled and fitted in one two-hour visit instead
of three appointments and two weeks in a temporary. The audience is adults who
have been burned by a surprise bill or a rushed appointment, plus nervous
patients who want to know exactly what will happen before they agree to it, so
the tone should be plain, specific and unembarrassed about money: prices,
minutes, named clinicians, no "state-of-the-art smile solutions."

Give the page a harbour identity: deep navy (#12263F) type and dark sections, a
coral accent (#F26D5B) for calls to action, a seafoam secondary (#2AA79B) for
supporting marks, all set on cool mist paper (#F1F5F7) rather than white, with
white cards, rounded corners, soft shadows, generous section padding and
alternating section backgrounds so the page has rhythm as you scroll. Every
piece of artwork must be inline SVG or a CSS gradient — a wave-and-tooth brand
mark, a substantial spot illustration in the hero, one icon per treatment
category, numbered step markers, portrait medallions for the four clinicians,
and accreditation marks in the trust bar. No network images anywhere.

Ship, top to bottom: a header with the brand mark, five destinations and a
"Book a visit" button; a hero with a headline, a twenty-plus-word subhead, two
calls to action and the illustration; a trust bar of accreditations and numbers;
**"The treatment index" — the published catalogue of twelve treatments as cards
with price and chair time, above them a row of category filter buttons (All,
Preventive, Cosmetic, Restorative, Emergency) and a live "showing N of 12"
count**; a two-block deep dive on the ceramic mill and the quiet-chair comfort
protocol; a numbered "your first visit" timeline; a clinician roster with names,
roles and bios; three membership plans with monthly prices; three named patient
stories; five real FAQs about cost, nerves, emergencies and children; a closing
call-to-action with an email capture; and a four-column footer with hours,
address, contact and legal links.

The treatment index is the interactive centrepiece: choosing a category must
narrow the catalogue to that category's treatments and nothing else, the count
must follow the list, and choosing **All** must bring the full twelve back. The
page opens on All.
