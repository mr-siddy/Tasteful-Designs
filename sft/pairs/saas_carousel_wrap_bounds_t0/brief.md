# Brief — Rooftree marketing landing page

Build the marketing landing page for **Rooftree**, a small B2B SaaS that gives
community land trusts and neighbourhood housing nonprofits one system for the
three things they currently keep in spreadsheets: their units and who lives in
them, the waitlist and income-qualification paperwork for every applicant, and
the quarterly reports their funders demand in seven incompatible formats.
Rooftree is built by a four-person team in Providence and sells to organisations
that own between twelve and four hundred homes — stewards, not landlords, with
one part-time bookkeeper and a board meeting every eight weeks. The voice should
be plain, warm and unmistakably competent: it respects that the reader's job is
housing people, not administering software, and it never says "leverage",
"empower" or "solutions". Concrete beats aspirational everywhere — name the
funder programs (HOME, CDBG, LIHTC recertification), name the file formats, name
the hours saved.

The page is a long-form landing page in a cool, editorial palette — near-white
`#F4F6FB` canvas, near-black `#131A2A` ink, indigo `#3D4EE0` as the primary and a
grounded green `#0E8F6F` as the accent — with generous section padding, a strong
type scale and varied section backgrounds so the page has rhythm as you scroll.
Every visual is inline SVG or a CSS gradient: a brand mark, an icon per
capability, a substantial hero illustration of the portfolio dashboard, partner
wordmarks in the trust bar, and spot illustrations in the deep-dive blocks. No
network images.

Ship these sections in order: a sticky header with a brand mark, five
destinations and a demo CTA; a hero with headline, a real subhead, two CTAs and
three proof stats; a trust bar of named partner organisations and adoption
numbers; six capability cards; three alternating deep-dive blocks that explain
compliance calendars, the applicant pipeline and funder reporting properly; an
integrations section for the systems it already talks to; a five-step rollout
timeline; **a customer-story carousel** as the centrepiece of the social proof —
one long story on screen at a time, previous/next arrows, dot indicators and a
"Story N of 5" counter, wrapping around at both ends; a short outcomes strip with
three attributed quotes; a six-question FAQ; a closing CTA with a real email
capture form; and a four-column footer with navigation, contact details, office
address and legal links.

Invent the customers and quote them by name and title — executive directors,
housing directors, stewardship managers at land trusts in real-sounding
American cities — with specific numbers in every story. No Lorem ipsum, no
placeholder names, no example.com.
