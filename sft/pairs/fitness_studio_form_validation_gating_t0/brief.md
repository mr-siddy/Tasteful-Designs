# Brief — Sablewood Movement Co. landing page

Build the marketing landing page for **Sablewood Movement Co.**, a 4,000-square-foot
reformer Pilates, heated yoga and strength studio on the top floor of a converted
rope-works building at 218 Delaney Wharf. It opened in 2017, caps every class at
twelve people, and its whole pitch is that you are coached by name rather than
shouted at from a podium — the founder, Tova Lindqvist, spent nine years in
physical therapy before she ever taught a class, and the studio takes people
coming back from a C-section, a torn ACL or fifteen years of sitting still just as
seriously as it takes the 5:45am regulars. Warm, grown-up, unintimidating: no
mirrors on the studio-two wall, no leaderboard, no shame about the beginner track.

The page is a single scrolling landing page in a warm palette — a deep spruce
green (`#1F4A3C`), a clay/terracotta accent (`#C4623A`), and a sand-toned neutral
ramp on an off-white `#FAF6F0` ground — with a heavy display-weight type scale,
generous section padding, 2xl radii, soft low shadows, and alternating section
backgrounds so the page has rhythm. Every image is inline SVG or a CSS gradient:
a mark for the header, a line-art reformer/rope-works illustration in the hero,
icons for the class cards and process steps, monogram portraits for the
instructors, and gradient-washed panels for the studio-tour blocks.

Ship, in order: a sticky header with the mark, five destinations and a "Book an
intro session" action; a hero with the headline, a real 30-word subhead, two CTAs
and three inline stats; a proof strip with the Google rating, member count, years
open and retention; a six-card class catalogue with length, intensity and drop-in
price; three membership tiers with real monthly prices and what each includes; a
three-block studio deep-dive covering the reformer room, the heated studio and
the recovery loft; a four-step "your first six weeks" on-ramp; a weekly schedule
strip; four named instructors with credentials and bios; three testimonials from
named members with their jobs; the intro-session booking form; a six-question
FAQ; a closing CTA; and a four-column footer with hours, address, contact and
legal.

The interaction the page is anchored on is the **intro-session booking form**
near the bottom. It collects a full name, an email address, a mobile number and a
preferred start week from a select, plus an optional note about injuries or
goals. It must refuse a submission that is not usable: submitting with fields
blank, or with an email that is not an address, or with a phone number that is
not a real ten-digit number, or with no start week chosen, keeps the visitor on
the form and prints the reason underneath each offending field — and the green
"you're booked" confirmation must not appear. Once everything is valid, the form
is replaced by that confirmation. Copy is specific throughout: real class names,
real prices, named people, a real-sounding phone number and address, and no
placeholder text anywhere.
