# Brief — Cinder & Salt (restaurant, landing page)

Build the marketing landing page for **Cinder & Salt**, a thirty-eight seat
wood-fire restaurant in the old Ropeworks building at 14 Harker Quay, Fenwick
Harbour. Everything is cooked over embers in a single open hearth: whole day-boat
fish from Delia Okoro's boat *The Wren*, salt-baked roots from Callowfield Market
Garden, flatbread from stone-ground Hallow Mill flour. Chef-owner **Noor Adeyemi**
cooked in Basque asadors for six years and came home to do the same thing with
cold-water fish; **Marta Vieira** runs the fire, **Ezra Kwan** does pastry and
ferments, **Priya Raval** runs the room and the cellar. There is an eight-seat
chef's counter facing the coals, and a private room upstairs — the Net Loft —
that seats fourteen at one table. Wednesday to Saturday, 5:30–10:30pm, plus a
Sunday harbour lunch. Reservations open on the first of the month; a third of the
room is held back for walk-ins at the bar. Prices are honest and printed. Phone
is (555) 0148.

The page should read warm, coastal and slightly smoky — cream paper, ember orange
and a deep harbour teal, generous section rhythm, real inline-SVG illustration
(the hearth, the quay, a producer map) rather than stock photography. Tone:
confident and specific, never precious; the kind of copy written by people who
actually know what time the boat lands.

**Sections, in order:** header with nav and a booking button; hero with the room's
promise and two calls to action; a credential bar (awards, rating, years, covers);
the hearth menu — six dishes with prices and a sentence each; the fire itself, in
two deep-dive blocks; the eight-seat chef's counter; the producers, mapped; how an
evening actually runs, in five numbered steps; the Net Loft private room with its
three configurations; the cellar, cider and no-alcohol pours; three named guest
reviews plus press lines; **an FAQ accordion of seven front-of-house questions,
where opening one answer closes whichever was already open**; hours, address,
transport and access details; a closing booking CTA; and a four-column footer.
