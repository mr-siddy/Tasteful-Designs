# Brief — Alder Court Dental (lead-gen landing page)

Build the appointment-request page for **Alder Court Dental**, an eleven-year-old
family and restorative dental studio at 22 Alder Court in the Kestrel Park
neighbourhood, run by principal dentist Dr. Nadia Rehman. Their whole positioning
is *unhurried, on time, and priced in writing before anything starts* — they run
one chair per dentist, book sixty-minute first visits, publish from-prices, and
answer the phone themselves. The page has to convert nervous adults who have put
off a check-up for years, parents booking two kids at once, and people in pain
today who need a same-day slot; the request form is the centre of the page, not
an afterthought at the bottom.

Ship a full long-form page: sticky header with the tooth-mark logo and a call
link, a hero that leads on "we run on time" with a supporting illustration and
two calls to action, a proof strip with the 4.9 rating, patient counts and the
insurers they bill directly, a six-service card grid with from-prices and visit
lengths, the **request-an-appointment form** with a sidebar of hours, address and
what to bring, three alternating deep-dive blocks on the intraoral scanner, the
in-house ceramics mill that makes single-visit crowns, and low-dose 3D imaging
plus comfort options, a five-step walkthrough of a first visit, the four-person
team with names and roles, three named patient testimonials, three membership
plans for people without insurance, six real FAQs about cost, nervousness,
children and emergencies, a closing call to action with hours and a map, and a
four-column footer.

The form itself asks for full name, email address, phone number, what the visit
is for, a preferred time of day, and an optional note. It must refuse to send an
incomplete or malformed request: every missing or bad field shows a short,
specific message directly under that field and the confirmation panel does not
appear until the whole request is valid. Only when everything checks out does the
form swap to a confirmation that greets the patient by first name and repeats
what they asked for.

Visual direction: deep teal and warm apricot on a warm stone neutral ramp, big
rounded cards, soft shadows, generous section padding, alternating section
backgrounds for rhythm. All artwork is inline SVG and CSS gradients — no network
images. Warm, plain-spoken copy; no clinical jargon without a translation.
