# Brief — Marigold Dental Co. (dental clinic, landing page)

Build the marketing landing page for **Marigold Dental Co.**, an independent
family-and-cosmetic dental practice at 12 Pennant Street in the Fernbank
neighbourhood, run by Dr. Priya Raghunathan since 2011. The practice's whole
pitch is *unhurried dentistry*: forty-minute appointments instead of twenty,
one clinician who stays with you from exam to crown fit, a published price list,
and same-day emergency slots held open every morning. The audience is anxious
adults who have put off the dentist for years and parents booking a first
checkup for a five-year-old, so the tone should be warm, plain-spoken and
concrete — prices, minutes, and named people, never "world-class care."

Give the page a marigold-and-pine identity: a warm gold accent (#E8A020) against
a deep pine green (#123B36), set on warm sand paper (#FBF7F0) rather than white,
with generous section padding, rounded cards, soft shadows and alternating
section backgrounds so the page has rhythm as you scroll. All artwork must be
inline SVG or CSS gradients — a tooth-and-leaf brand mark, a spot illustration
in the hero, an icon per service, portrait medallions for the clinicians, and
accreditation marks in the trust bar. No network images.

Ship, top to bottom: a sticky header with a brand mark, five destinations and a
"Book a visit" button; a hero with a headline, a real subhead, two calls to
action and the illustration; a trust bar of accreditations and numbers; a
six-card treatment price grid; **a "Care pathways" section where four tabs —
Preventive, Cosmetic, Restorative and Emergency — each swap in their own panel
of treatments, timings and prices**; a deep-dive on the chair-side comfort
protocol and the in-house ceramic mill; a numbered "your first visit" timeline;
a clinician roster with names, roles and bios; three membership plans with
monthly prices; three named patient stories; five real FAQs about cost, nerves,
children and emergencies; a closing call-to-action with an email capture; and a
four-column footer with hours, address, contact and legal links.

The care-pathways tabs are the interactive centrepiece: selecting a tab must
show that pathway's panel and only that one, and must announce the selection to
assistive technology. The page opens on Preventive.
