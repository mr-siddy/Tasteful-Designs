# Brief — Foundry Row Strength Club (fitness_studio · landing)

Build the marketing landing page for **Foundry Row Strength Club**, a 6,200 sq ft
barbell-and-conditioning studio at 129 Foundry Row, in the Ironside district
between the old rail yard and the canal. It was opened in 2019 by Marisol Enders,
a former collegiate throws coach, and Dev Achterberg, a physio who got tired of
sending people back to gyms that would re-injure them. The club is not a
big-box gym and not a bootcamp: every member trains on a written program, group
sizes are capped at eight, and the barbell is the centre of the room. The voice
should be warm, plain-spoken and slightly blue-collar — coaches who measure
things, no hype, no "crush it", no shouting. Roughly 340 members, average
tenure 19 months, five coaches, a strength floor with twelve platforms, a
conditioning bay with rowers/bikes/skis, and a small rehab annex.

The page runs long: a sticky header with the club mark and six destinations, a
hero with a real subhead and two calls to action, a proof strip (rating, member
count, coach credentials, partner marks), a six-card program grid (Barbell
Foundations, Engine Room, Hybrid, Masters 50+, Return to Strength, Open Floor)
each with a schedule and a sentence of real description, the **membership
pricing section** with a monthly/annual billing toggle, three alternating
deep-dive blocks explaining how coaching actually works (assess, program,
retest), a facility tour with the equipment list and the staffed-hours table, a
four-step on-ramp, a four-coach roster with credentials, three named
testimonials, a six-question FAQ, a closing call to action, and a four-column
footer with address, hours, nav and legal.

**The membership section is the piece that carries the rule.** Three tiers sit
side by side in this order: **Open Floor** ($89/mo, $79/mo billed annually) for
self-programmed members who just want the room; **Coached** ($179/mo, $159/mo
annually) for three coached small-group sessions a week plus a written program;
and **Full Program** ($289/mo, $259/mo annually) for unlimited sessions, monthly
1:1 reviews and nutrition support. **Coached is the plan the club promotes** —
about two thirds of new members land on it and it is what the coaches quote
first — so exactly one card on the whole page wears the "Most popular" tag and
the raised dark treatment, and it is the Coached card, the middle one of the
three. Open Floor and Full Program stay plain. Flipping the billing toggle
between monthly and annual changes every price but must not move the promotion.

Visual identity: ink `#14181D`, ember `#D9481F` as the brand accent, pine
`#12554D` as the secondary, on a warm sand neutral ramp (`#FBF7F1`, `#F2EAE0`,
`#E2D6C6`, `#7A6E60`). Heavy tracking-tight display type against quiet body
copy, generous section padding, alternating section backgrounds for rhythm,
consistent 2xl radii and soft shadows. No network is available, so every visual
is inline SVG or a CSS gradient — a club mark, an icon set, a hero illustration
of a loaded barbell over a gradient field, a floor-plan diagram, partner marks
and coach portrait medallions. No stock photography, no placeholder copy.
