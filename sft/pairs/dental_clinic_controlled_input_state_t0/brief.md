# Brief — Lantern Lane Dental (dental_clinic · landing)

Build the marketing landing page for **Lantern Lane Dental**, a four-chair general and
restorative practice at 42 Lantern Lane in Kelso Park, open since 1998 and now run by
Dr. Ines Okonkwo out of a converted lamp-works with a saw-tooth roof and north light over
every chair. The practice's whole pitch is *unhurried*: appointments are booked at forty
minutes rather than the industry's twenty, quotes are written before anything is drilled,
and nobody is upsold a veneer they did not ask about. The page should read warm, plain and
slightly old-fashioned — the tone of a good letter, not of a clinic brochure. Palette is a
deep ink-navy (`#152046` / `#2b3a7a`) on warm sand (`#faf6f0`) with a single coral accent
(`#f2704a`), a heavy display headline against light body text, `rounded-3xl` cards with
soft shadows, and alternating section backgrounds so the scroll has rhythm. Every visual is
drawn as inline SVG or a CSS gradient — a lantern brand mark, a saw-tooth-roof hero
illustration with a hanging lamp, one line-art icon per treatment, geometric clinician
portraits, a small street map beside the booking form, and a ribbon divider under the hero.

The centrepiece of the page — the section everything else funnels into — is **"Ask us for a
time"**, a request form the visitor actually fills in rather than a mailto link: full name,
email, phone, a treatment dropdown, a preferred-time dropdown and a free-text note, each one
a properly labelled control whose value the page keeps hold of as it is typed. It has to
*feel* alive: the line under the fields updates to name the person as soon as they type their
name, the note field counts characters against its 300-character ceiling, and sending the
request swaps in a confirmation that reads their own details back to them — the name they
typed, the treatment they chose, the time they asked for — before the form empties itself
ready for the next visitor. Around it: sticky header with six destinations and a phone
number; a hero (`Careful dentistry on Lantern Lane`) with two calls to action; a trust band
carrying the 4.9/1,142 rating, 26 years and 7,800 patients; six treatments with real
from-prices ($95 emergency through $4,950 implants); a three-block technology deep-dive on
the intraoral scanner, the in-house crown mill and low-dose 3D imaging; the four-step first
visit; four named clinicians with credentials; three Lantern Care memberships at $29/$49/$89
a month; three named patient stories; six real questions with real answers; a closing CTA;
and a four-column footer with hours, address and legal. Aim for something a practice manager
would sign off on: 8+ sections, 650+ words of specific copy, zero placeholder text.
