# Brief — Bramble Court Dental (dental_clinic · landing)

Build the marketing landing page for **Bramble Court Dental**, an eleven-chair
independent general and cosmetic practice at 22 Bramble Court in the Wyeford
Quarter, run since 2009 by principal dentist Dr. Maren Okonkwo. Their entire
positioning is *the price is on the wall*: every fee the practice charges is
printed on a board in the waiting room and published on this page, nobody is
treated without a written quote they have signed, treatment plans over four
hundred pounds can be spread interest-free over ten months, and one emergency
chair is held empty until three o'clock every weekday for registered patients. The
page has three people to convert: an adult who has avoided dentists for years
because they are frightened of the bill as much as the drill, a parent who wants
both children seen in one trip on a Saturday morning, and someone with a broken
tooth right now who needs to know they can be seen today.

Ship a full long-form page in this order: a header carrying the bramble-and-tooth
mark, five destinations, the phone number and a "Book a check-up" action; a hero
that leads on the published-price promise with an inline SVG illustration of the
price board, two calls to action and a strip of four quick facts; a proof bar with
the 4.9 rating, the review count, years open, registered patients and the insurers
billed directly; a six-card treatment grid with from-prices and appointment
lengths; **the published fee schedule** — a real table of twelve line items with
prices and what each one includes, plus the payment-plan panel beside it; three
alternating deep-dive blocks on the quiet room for nervous patients, the on-site
milling lab that finishes a crown in one visit, and Saturday family mornings; a
five-step walkthrough of a first visit; the four-person clinical team with names,
roles and one-line biographies; three named patient stories; three membership
plans for patients without insurance; a weekly opening-hours table with parking,
bus and step-free access notes; **the FAQ accordion**; a closing call to action;
and a four-column footer.

The FAQ block near the bottom is the interactive centrepiece. It is headed
"Questions the front desk hears every week" and holds seven real questions —
knowing the cost up front, coming back after years away, being seen the same day
with a broken tooth, how insurance is claimed, what happens if you are frightened
of the chair, bringing two children to one appointment, and where to park on
Bramble Court. Each question is a full-width button with a plus icon that rotates
when it opens, and each answer is a proper two-to-three-sentence paragraph naming
a number, a time or a person — the parking answer names the Halyard Yard car park
behind the building. The accordion opens **one answer at a time**: opening a
question closes whichever answer was already open, clicking the open question
closes it again, and the block loads with everything collapsed.

Visual direction: deep indigo `#332A56` and a coral `#E4735B` accent over a warm
oat neutral ramp (`#FBF9F5` page, `#F1ECE3` alternate sections, `#E3DCD0` rules,
`#6A6255` body copy), with one full-width indigo block for contrast. Large rounded
cards, soft shadows, generous section padding, a type scale with real weight
contrast, and alternating section backgrounds so the page has rhythm as you
scroll. All artwork is inline SVG and CSS gradients — icons, the practice mark,
the price-board illustration, the lab illustration, the team portraits and the
access map — with no network images anywhere. Copy is warm, plain-spoken and free
of clinical jargon; every claim names a number, a time or a person.
