# Brief — fitness_responsive_grid_t0

**Industry:** fitness_studio · **Archetype:** landing · **Seed:** responsive_grid_collapse

Build the marketing landing page for **Northline Strength Co.**, a small-group
strength-and-conditioning studio at 42 Cavendish Lane, Brunswick East, Melbourne,
founded in 2019 by Priya Raghunathan after eight years as a strength coach for
Rowing Victoria. It is deliberately not a gym: no mirrors wall, no membership
treadmill, no 40-person classes — nine lifters per session, three coaches on the
floor, and a barbell programme written for people who have jobs, kids and knees
that are forty years old. The tone should be plain-spoken, technical where it
matters and completely free of hype: "you will be shown how to hinge before you
are shown how to deadlift" rather than "unlock your potential". Commit to an ink
and ember identity — near-black `stone-950` surfaces, an amber accent used
sparingly for actions and intensity tags, limestone neutrals for the light
sections, heavy tracking-tight display type against small uppercase eyebrows —
and alternate light and dark section backgrounds so the page has rhythm as you
scroll. Every visual is inline SVG or a CSS gradient: the wordmark, the section
icons, the barbell/plate hero illustration, the method diagrams and the coach
portrait medallions. Ship a sticky header with five destinations and a trial CTA;
a hero with the studio name, a promise and two calls to action; a proof bar of
membership, class-cap, tenure and review numbers plus partner marks; a programme
list of six offerings with real weekly prices; **a "this week on the floor" class
schedule section built as a card grid of six named classes plus a four-tile
intensity-level key — this is the section the responsive rule is anchored on, so
the grid must stack to one column on a phone and open to multiple columns from
the `md` breakpoint up**; three alternating deep-dive blocks on the method
(assessment, the block cycle, the load ladder); a numbered four-step first-month
process and a paragraph about the room itself; three coaches with credentials;
three member stories with named attribution (a teacher from Coburg, a bike
courier, a retired nurse); six real FAQs about pricing, injuries, parking and
class caps; a closing CTA with a trial-booking email field; and a four-column
footer with navigation, contact, staffed hours and legal lines. Aim for a page a
studio owner would actually pay for: twelve landmark sections, 650+ words of
specific copy, no stock-photo shaped holes and no placeholder text anywhere.
