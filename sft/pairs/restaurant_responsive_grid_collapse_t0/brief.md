# Brief — Persimmon Alley (restaurant, landing page)

Build the single-page marketing site for **Persimmon Alley**, a Sichuan–Taiwanese
night-market kitchen in a converted letterpress print shop at 22 Dunbar Lane, in
the Ashgrove Works district. Chef-owner **Wen Tso-lin** cooks off a six-ring wok
range and a charcoal skewer trough; her business partner **Dai Okonjo** runs the
front room and a tea-and-mezcal list nobody expected. The personality is loud,
late and unfussy: paper lanterns over concrete, enamel plates, numbers chalked on
the old proofing press, service until 1am Thursday to Saturday. It is a walk-in
room for two thirds of its seats and takes bookings for the rest.

The page must sell three things in this order: **the board** (the rotating
night-market plates people actually come for), **the two set feasts** (Alley
Feast and Full Lantern, priced per person), and **the room itself** (the wok
range, the fermentation cupboard, the noodle bench). Write the copy in the
restaurant's own voice — real dish names with real prices and one honest sentence
each, real opening hours, real heat ratings, named guests in the testimonials,
and answers to the questions a walk-in actually asks (parking, spice, kids,
allergies, large groups, the wait).

Ship it as a long-form React + Tailwind landing page: a sticky header with five
destinations and a booking action; a hero with a lantern illustration; a proof
bar with press and ratings; **the night-market board section, which is a card
grid of six plates plus four skewer tiles**; the set-feast menus; three
alternating deep-dive blocks on the fire, the ferment and the noodles; the room
and hours with a floor-plan graphic; the kitchen team; three named testimonials;
a five-question FAQ; a closing reservation block with a real enquiry form; and a
four-column footer.

Visual direction: ink-black and warm charcoal grounds, **persimmon orange**
(#E4572E) as the single loud accent, a jade (#2F7A6B) secondary, cream #FBF5EC
type on dark and ink type on cream so the page alternates rhythm as you scroll.
All artwork must be inline SVG or CSS gradients — lanterns, flame marks, a chilli
heat scale, a wok spiral, the floor plan — because the page ships with no
network. Generous section padding, one radius language, real weight contrast
between display and body type.

**Layout rule this page must obey:** every card grid on the page is a single
column on a phone and only opens into multiple columns from the `md` breakpoint
up — the night-market board included, since that is the section people scroll to
on a phone while standing outside deciding whether to queue.
