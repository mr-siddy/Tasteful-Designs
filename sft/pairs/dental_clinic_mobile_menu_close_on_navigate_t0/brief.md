# Brief — Foundry Row Dental (dental_clinic · landing)

Build the marketing landing page for **Foundry Row Dental**, a nine-chair general and
restorative dental practice at 12 Kilnwood Street in the Foundry Quarter — a converted
1908 brass works with skylights, exposed steel and a coffee bar in the waiting room.
The practice is owner-run by Dr. Priya Raman and Dr. Marcus Ovalle, opens at 6:45am for
before-work appointments, mills its own same-day ceramic crowns on site, and prices every
treatment publicly because the neighbourhood is full of people who have been surprised by
a dental bill before. The voice is warm, plain-spoken and specific: it names streets,
quotes real dollar figures, admits how long things take, and never says "state of the art".
The audience is Foundry Quarter locals aged 25–55 — tradespeople, studio owners, young
families — who are nervous about cost and about dentists generally, and who will read this
page on a phone on the bus.

The page should carry, top to bottom: a sticky header with the brass-mark logo, five
destinations, a phone number and a "Book a chair" action, plus a hamburger that opens a
full-screen destination panel on phones; a hero with the practice name, a real subhead and
two calls to action over a warm gradient with an inline SVG skyline of the old works; a
trust bar of hard numbers (years open, chairs, same-day crowns milled, review score); a
treatment menu of six services with flat prices, durations and a sentence of honest
explanation each; a deep dive on the in-house ceramic mill that explains why one visit
replaces three; a second deep dive on how the practice handles dental anxiety, with a
named comfort menu; a five-step "your first visit" timeline; a roster of the three
clinicians with credentials and something human about each; a three-tier membership plan
for people without insurance, with the middle tier marked as the one most people choose;
three testimonials attributed to named locals with their occupations; hours, parking,
transit and step-free access; a six-question FAQ answering cost, insurance, emergencies,
kids, nervous patients and parking; a closing call to action; and a four-column footer
with treatments, the practice, contact details and legal links.

Visual identity: deep pine `#14312c` and copper `#d9682f` on a warm bone-white ramp
(`#fbf6f0` page, `#f3e9dd` panels), serif headings against a sans body, generous section
padding, `rounded-2xl` cards with soft shadows, and alternating section backgrounds so the
page has rhythm. Every icon, logo, illustration and divider is inline SVG — no network is
available, so no `http(s)` image URLs anywhere.

**The interaction this page is anchored on:** on a phone, tapping the hamburger opens the
destination panel over the page; tapping any destination inside it — or the booking button
inside it — must take you there *and* dismiss the panel, leaving nothing covering the page.
