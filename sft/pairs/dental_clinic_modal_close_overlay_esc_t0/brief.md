# Brief — Alder & Bay Dental Studio (landing page)

Build the marketing landing page for **Alder & Bay Dental Studio**, a nine-person
family and cosmetic dental practice at 5412 Ballard Avenue NW in Seattle, opened
in 2014 by Dr. Nadia Okonkwo in a converted 1920s ship-chandlery storefront. The
practice sells calm, not urgency: same-day CEREC crowns milled in the back room,
a hygiene team that books forty-minute appointments instead of twenty, weighted
blankets and noise-cancelling headphones in every operatory, and transparent
posted prices for people who pay out of pocket. It draws boat builders from
Fishermen's Terminal, night-shift nurses, and Ballard families who have been
coming since the practice had one chair. The tone should read like a well-made
neighbourhood shop — warm, specific, unhurried, faintly nautical — never like a
dental-marketing template. Commit to a palette of harbour navy `#0B2540`, copper
`#C2703D` and sage `#4E7C6A` on a warm sand `#FAF6F1`, with a real type scale,
generous section padding, and every icon, badge and illustration drawn as inline
SVG (no network is available, so no remote images).

Ship the full page, wide and shallow — one self-contained component per section,
flat tree: a sticky header with brand mark, five destinations and a phone number;
a hero with an exam-room illustration and a $149 new-patient offer; a trust strip
of accepted plans and practice stats; a posted price list of six treatments; a
**new-patient booking section whose "Reserve a new-patient visit" button opens a
modal dialog** holding the request form, the three next-available slots and the
studio's direct line; a three-block deep dive on chairside comfort, the CEREC
mill and sedation; a five-step walkthrough of a first visit; the four-person care
team with portrait illustrations; three membership plans priced monthly for
uninsured patients; three named patient testimonials; six real FAQs about
insurance, parking, anxiety and emergencies; a closing call to action; and a
four-column footer with hours, address, services and legal links.

The booking dialog is the interactive centre of the page: it must open from the
section's primary button and be dismissible the three ways people actually
expect — the close control, a click on the dimmed overlay behind it, and the
Escape key — leaving the page exactly as it was underneath.
