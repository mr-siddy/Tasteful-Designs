# Brief — Cinder & Rye (restaurant, landing page)

Build the single-page marketing site for **Cinder & Rye**, a live-fire Basque-influenced
asador and bakehouse that opened in a former tobacco drying shed at 214 Ferris Street in
the Brightleaf district of Durham, North Carolina. Everything savoury comes off one
eighteen-foot oak-and-hickory grill built by chef-owner **Inés Arrieta**, who cooked seven
years on the coast outside San Sebastián before moving south; everything else comes out of
the bakehouse that head baker **Emmett Sandoval** fires at 4am, which is why the dining
room smells like burnt rye before it smells like smoke. Beverage director **Priya Raghavan**
pours txakoli, Jura, and a cider list poured from the shoulder. The room seats fifty-two,
plus **the Long Table** — a single twenty-two-foot slab of reclaimed heart pine along the
south wall that the restaurant rents out for parties of six to sixty. The voice is warm,
plainspoken and specific: real prices, real hours, real names, no chef-speak.

The page must run long — a full scroll, not a teaser. It needs: a sticky header with a
brand mark and five destinations; a hero with an ember-gradient background and a large
inline-SVG illustration of the grill; a trust strip of press and awards; a six-dish menu
board off the fire with prices and one honest sentence each; a bakehouse section for the
4am side of the building; a three-block deep dive on the wood, the grill and the farms;
**a private-dining inquiry form for the Long Table** — name, email, party size and which
seating, plus an optional note — that refuses anything it cannot actually act on and tells
the visitor which field needs attention; a numbered walk-through of how a Long Table
evening actually runs; a section on the room and the people in it; three testimonials from
named guests with roles; a six-question FAQ; a visit block with hours, address and transit;
a closing CTA; and a multi-column footer.

Design it like a restaurant that spent money on its identity: charcoal ink `#1b1512`, ember
`#b8451f`, rye gold `#e0912f` and a cream neutral ramp on `#faf5ec`, a real type scale with
heavy weight contrast, generous section padding, alternating section backgrounds so the
scroll has rhythm, and a consistent radius and shadow language. No network is available, so
every visual is inline SVG or a CSS gradient — the grill illustration, the wheat and flame
icons, the section dividers, the seating-plan diagram and the neighbourhood map are all
hand-drawn in markup. Semantic landmarks and real labels throughout; the form controls are
properly associated with their labels and the error messages sit under the field they
belong to.
