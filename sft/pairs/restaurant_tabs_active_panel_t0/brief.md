# Brief — Casa Marisol (restaurant · landing)

Build the marketing landing page for **Casa Marisol**, a forty-eight-seat Catalan
live-fire restaurant at 118 Rope Walk in the Larkspur District of Providence,
Rhode Island, opened in 2019 by chef Núria Vidal-Reyes and named for her
grandmother. Everything the kitchen sends out has touched an open oak hearth that
is lit at 5:40 every morning: whole turbot on the coals, bomba rice cooked until
the base catches, peppers buried overnight in the ember box, bread pulled from
the ash. The page has to sell that single idea — one fire, cooked all day — to
two audiences at once: a couple deciding where to eat on Friday, and a guest
booking the upstairs loft for a birthday. It should open on a dark, ember-lit
hero over a stone-and-amber palette with a serif display face, then move through
credentials (a 2024 Restaurant of the Year nod, a 2025 James Beard semifinalist
listing, a 4.8 guest rating), the six plates the room is known for with real
prices, and a **tabbed menu section where the guest picks between Lunch, Dinner,
Bar and Sunday Rice and reads only the one they chose** — the section that has to
work on a phone without becoming a wall of dishes. After that: a three-part
deep-dive on how the fire actually works (the cooperage oak, the ember box, the
brine), the five named suppliers the kitchen buys from, a timestamped walk
through one service day, the private-dining options with capacities and prices,
three testimonials from named guests, six real FAQs about walk-ins, allergies and
parking, a closing reservation block with a working request form, and a
four-column footer carrying hours, address, phone and email. Copy should sound
like the restaurant wrote it — specific, unfussy, a little proud of the fire.
No stock photography is available, so every visual is inline SVG or a CSS
gradient: a brand mark, an arched hearth illustration in the hero, icons on every
plate, award badges, a loft floorplan.
