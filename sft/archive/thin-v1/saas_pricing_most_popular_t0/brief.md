# Brief — Slatepine (SaaS landing page)

Build the marketing landing page for **Slatepine**, a shared-inbox and helpdesk
SaaS that pulls email, live chat and web forms into one threaded inbox for small
support teams. The page opens with a hero that names the product, states the
promise in one sentence and points at a free trial, followed by a quiet strip of
customer logos and three short value propositions (one thread per customer,
rule-based routing, saved replies and a shared knowledge base). The centrepiece
is a per-seat pricing section with exactly three tiers laid out left to right —
**Starter** at $12, **Team** at $29 and **Scale** at $58, each with a one-line
audience blurb, a feature list and its own call to action. Sales wants the
middle tier, Team, called out as the recommended plan: it alone wears a "Most
popular" ribbon and gets the highlighted card treatment (accented border, filled
button), while Starter and Scale sit as plain cards. Close with a contentinfo
footer carrying the company line and address. Calm, typographic, slate-and-teal
palette; Tailwind only, no images, no network calls, no timers — the page is
fully static and deterministic.
