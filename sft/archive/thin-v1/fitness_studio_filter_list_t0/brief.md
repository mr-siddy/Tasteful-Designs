# Brief — Ridgeline Studio landing page

Build a one-page landing site for **Ridgeline Studio**, a small-group strength and
conditioning gym on Alder Street that caps every class at ten people and coaches all
of them in person. The page should open with a calm, high-contrast hero — an eyebrow
line placing the studio, the studio name as the page's single `<h1>`, a sentence of
positioning copy about coach-led training rather than a room full of machines, and a
primary call to action inviting a free intro week that jumps down to the schedule —
followed by a thin band of three proof points (class cap, coaches on the floor,
classes per week). The centrepiece is **this week's class schedule**: a row of filter
buttons — All, Strength, Conditioning, Mobility — above a list of the eight named
classes, each card showing the class name, its type, the day and start time, the
session length and the coach's name, plus a line under the buttons reading
"Showing N of 8 classes". Pressing a type narrows the list to just that type and
updates the count; the currently chosen button reads as pressed to a screen reader;
pressing All brings the whole eight-class week back. Close with a footer carrying the
address, the staffed hours and a phone number. Warm neutral palette — stone and
slate with a single lime accent for the active state — generous whitespace, semantic
markup throughout.
