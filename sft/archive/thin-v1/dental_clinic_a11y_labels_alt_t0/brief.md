# Brief — Alder & Vine Dental (landing page)

Build a single-page marketing site for **Alder & Vine Dental**, a three-chair family
and cosmetic dental practice on Kingsmead Row that has been open since 2009. The tone
should be calm and unhurried — the opposite of a clinical stock-photo dentist site —
with a sage-and-teal palette, generous whitespace, and rounded cards on a warm off-white
background. Open with a slim announcement bar offering same-day emergency slots that the
visitor can dismiss, then a sticky header carrying the practice name and three compact
icon-only controls: call the front desk, get directions to the Kingsmead Row entrance,
and jump to the booking form. The hero pairs a short promise ("Dentistry that runs on
time") with a photograph of the reception room, a line about evening and Saturday
appointments, and a primary call to action. Below it, a "Who you'll see" section
introduces the three clinicians — Dr. Mara Ellison (principal dentist, implants and
restorative), Dr. Tobias Frey (cosmetic and orthodontics), and Imogen Vance (lead
hygienist) — each as a portrait card with a photo, a role line, and one sentence of
plain-English biography. Finish with a "Book an appointment" panel: a short request form
asking for full name, email, phone, preferred day, and what the visit is for, plus a
submit button and a reassurance line that the front desk replies within one working day;
then a footer with the address, phone number and opening hours. Everything on the page
must be announceable by a screen reader — every photograph carries alt text naming what
it shows, every icon-only button carries an accessible name, and every form control is
wired to its visible label — because a good share of the practice's patients browse with
assistive technology. No network calls, no timers, no randomness; the form only needs to
swap in a confirmation message on submit.
